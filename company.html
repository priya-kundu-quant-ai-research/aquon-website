About Aquon Inc.

Aquon Inc. is a technology company on a mission to build trustworthy optimization infrastructure for high-impact decision systems. We believe that optimization systems powering critical decisions—from capital markets to energy grids to global supply chains—deserve computational infrastructure built on trust, not just speed.

Our approach is to embed correctness, auditability, and reproducibility into the computational foundation itself through the Aqua programming language—a new class of compiler for decision systems. Zeton, our first product, validates this technology in professional asset management, one of the most demanding environments where performance, trust, and speed are non-negotiable.

Our Mission

To build trustworthy optimization infrastructure that embeds correctness, auditability, and reproducibility into the computational foundation of decision systems.

Our Vision

Finance validates the engine—society benefits from the platform. Beyond portfolio management, we envision trustworthy optimization infrastructure enabling reproducible computational science, safer automated decision systems, and democratized access to optimization for climate simulation, public policy, and critical infrastructure.
