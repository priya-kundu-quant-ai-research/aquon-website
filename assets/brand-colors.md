## Usage Guidelines
- Primary background: White (#FFFFFF)
- Hero sections: Use gradient backgrounds sparingly
- CTAs: Teal (#1DBFBF) with white text
- Secondary CTAs: Dark Blue (#0D3D4D) with white text
- Accent elements: Purple (#8B3FBF)
- Text: Dark Gray (#212529) for primary, Medium Gray (#6C757D) for secondary
